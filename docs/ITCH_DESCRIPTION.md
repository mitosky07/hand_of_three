# Hand of Three

Hand of Three is an after-hours card duel built around Karjitsu: Rock, Paper and Scissors reimagined as an endless run across a worn video-poker table.

Every match is best of three. Choose from a five-card hand, weigh elemental advantage against card level, and outplay the Oracle before it closes your run. Victories pay chips that can be spent between matches on temporary upgrades, tactical items and rare relics. Lose the match and the table resets to round one, but your record and unlocked cosmetics remain.

## What is at the table

- Endless single-player runs with escalating Oracle opponents
- Best-of-three card matches with levels and card keywords
- Chips, temporary upgrades, tactical items and relic combinations
- Optional contracts that change how a match should be approached
- Seeded daily runs with the same opening deal for every player
- Local two-player play on one device
- Private online rooms through the companion WebSocket server
- Unlockable felts, card backs, chips, stamps and portraits
- Element finishers: Rock crushes, Paper wraps and Scissors slices
- Persistent best round and play-time log
- Keyboard, mouse and touch support with a reduced-motion option

## Controls

### Menus

- `WASD` or Arrow Keys — Move
- `Enter` or `Space` — Select

### At the table

- `A` / `D` or Left / Right — Choose a card
- `W`, Up, Enter or Space — Confirm
- `S` or Down — Cancel
- `1–5` — Choose a card directly
- `I` — Open run items
- `Escape` — Pause
